DATASET SOURCE
===============

Project: HR Workforce & Attrition Analytics

Dataset used:
IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance-style dataset
Common filename: WA_Fn-UseC_-HR-Employee-Attrition.csv

Source reference:
Kaggle dataset commonly titled “IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance”.
Dataset page: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset

Important attribution and usage notes:
- Verify the exact source page and its current license/terms before redistributing the CSV.
- This dataset is commonly used as a fictional/synthetic HR analytics dataset for learning and portfolio demonstrations.
- Do not describe the rows as actual IBM employee records or claim that the analysis represents IBM's real workforce.
- If you obtained the CSV from another source, replace the link above with the exact source you used.
- If redistribution is not permitted, do not commit the CSV to GitHub. Provide the source link instead.

Dataset facts used in this project:
- Expected row count in the commonly distributed version: 1,470 employees.
- Common fields include EmployeeNumber, Age, Gender, Department, JobRole, MonthlyIncome,
  YearsAtCompany, OverTime, JobSatisfaction, EnvironmentSatisfaction, WorkLifeBalance,
  PerformanceRating, and Attrition.
- The standard version does not contain a genuine Hire Date field. Do not fabricate one.
